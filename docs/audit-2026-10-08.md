## External link checks and remaining limitations

The initial GET check covered 84 unique HTTPS URLs, including URLs in event rules that are not currently displayed. 58 returned HTTP 200 after redirects. An HTTP 200 does not guarantee a service is fully usable; the closed volunteer form was one such result.

One confirmed 404 was repaired. Both replacement affiliate lookup and Earth Day events map returned HTTP 200. Several destinations refused automated requests or timed out; they remain unverified rather than being classified as dead or removed. Their URLs and raw results are in `link-check-2026-10-08.json`.

All five connected Verve N Veda game/explorer app URLs and all nine linked Verve N Veda network URLs returned HTTP 200 after redirects to `vervenveda.com`. Complete external game flows, third-party sign-in, registrations, and submissions were not tested or performed.
