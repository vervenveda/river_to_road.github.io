Stewardship and activism safety

Protect people while protecting the environment

Responsible action is lawful, informed, nonviolent, accessible, and prepared for local hazards.

Permission & jurisdiction

Confirm who owns or manages the site and whether permits, waivers, insurance, or notices are required.

Traffic & visibility

Avoid active roadways unless an authorized agency provides a safe program, plan, equipment, and supervision.

Hazardous materials

Do not handle needles, chemicals, explosives, unknown containers, dead animals, or dangerous waste. Mark the area and contact authorities.

Heat, weather & wildlife

Use water, shade, sun protection, weather awareness, appropriate footwear, and local wildlife precautions.

Youth & accessibility

Match tasks to age and ability, supervise minors, offer accessible roles, and provide clear boundaries and rest areas.

Privacy & documentation

Avoid publishing faces, license plates, addresses, or sensitive information without permission.

Public participation

Read source documents, preserve uncertainty, follow meeting rules, and distinguish facts from claims or opinions.

Peaceful civic action

Use lawful, nonviolent methods and confirm organizer identity, event safety, costs, and expectations before attending.
